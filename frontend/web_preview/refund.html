We take pride in delivering spotless solar washing. If things don't go as expected, here is our transparent refund policy.

1. Booking Cancellation Policy

  • Free Cancellation: You can cancel any booking free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled appointment slot directly through the app (My Bookings tab) or by contacting support.
  • Late Cancellation: If cancelled within 2 hours of the slot or after the technician has arrived on-site, a nominal inspection/dispatch fee of ₹99 may be deducted if online prepaid.

2. Rescheduling Appointments

Rescheduling is completely free! You can reschedule your booking date or time slot by contacting customer support at solwashbussiness@gmail.com or calling +91 98765 43210.

3. Weather & Safety Delays

In case of severe weather events (heavy storms, lightning, extreme rain), our team will contact you to reschedule the appointment at your earliest convenience without any penalty.

4. Refund Eligibility & Criteria

A 100% full refund will be granted in the following scenarios:

  • Booking cancelled by the customer before technician dispatch.
  • Service cancellation by SolWash due to operational constraints or weather delays where rescheduling is declined.
  • Duplicate or excess deduction caused by technical network issues during Razorpay checkout.
  • Substandard service quality validated upon immediate inspection within 24 hours of service.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

All approved refunds are initiated immediately through our payment gateway (Razorpay). The amount is credited directly back to the original source account (UPI / Debit / Credit Card / Bank Account) within 5 to 7 business days depending on your issuing bank.

6. Contacting Support for Refund Inquiries

To request or track a refund, simply email us with your Order Number (e.g. SOL-XXXXXX-XXXX):

  • Email: solwashbussiness@gmail.com
  • Helpline: +91 98765 43210
  • Business Hours: Monday – Saturday, 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST
