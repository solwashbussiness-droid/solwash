This Privacy Policy applies to the SolWash Mobile App (com.solwash.app) and the official website at https://solwash.in.

1. Introduction

Welcome to SolWash ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by SolWash Solar Care Services. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, sharing, and security when you use our Android application and related web services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the necessary information required to facilitate rooftop solar cleaning, order scheduling, and customer support:

A. Personal Information Provided by You

  • Account Data: Your full name, email address, phone number, and account credentials when you register, log in via OTP, or sign in using Google.
  • Service & Address Details: Your physical residential or commercial rooftop address where solar washing is to be performed.
  • Profile Data: Your selected profile avatar and app preferences.

B. Location Data (GPS Coordinates)

When you place an order or use the "Detect Current Location" feature, SolWash requests permission to access your device's precise and/or approximate location. This data is collected solely to:

  • Auto-fill and verify your service location address.
  • Assign the nearest trained solar washing technician to your rooftop installation.

Note: We do not track your location in the background when the app is closed.

C. Transaction & Payment Information

When you pay online via UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or NetBanking, transactions are securely processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited, an RBI-authorized payment aggregator. SolWash does not store or process your bank account passwords, card CVVs, or full credit/debit card numbers on its servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To schedule, confirm, execute, and inspect solar panel washing bookings.
  • To send transactional notifications, OTP codes, order invoices, and real-time status updates via SMS, in-app notifications, and email.
  • To customize your experience and display your profile information.
  • To prevent fraud, verify accounts, and comply with applicable laws.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

We partner with trusted third-party service providers who process data strictly under confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Identity Services / OAuth: For secure single-click authentication.
  • Razorpay Payment Gateway: For PCI-DSS compliant digital payment processing.
  • DiceBear Avatars API: For rendering generated profile avatars without sharing personal data.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement strict technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS (TLS 1.3).
  • Passwords are salted and cryptographically hashed with bcrypt before storage.
  • We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide services or satisfy legal obligations.

6. User Rights & Account / Data Deletion Request (Google Play Policy Compliant)

In accordance with Google Play Store User Data policies, you have the full right to access, update, or request permanent deletion of your SolWash account and all associated personal data.

How to request account & data deletion:

  1. Send an email to our designated privacy team at: solwashbussiness@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Account & Data Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email/Phone]
  3. Our verification team will confirm your identity and permanently purge your account record, order history, profile avatars, and address data within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

SolWash does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our services are strictly intended for property owners and individuals aged 18 and above.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect policy or operational changes. The updated version will always be accessible at https://solwash.in/privacy.
